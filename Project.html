01 — Manuscripts in Preparation

Current Research Output

Manuscript in Preparation

HPV Vaccination Hesitancy Among Adolescent Girls in Rural and Urban School Settings in Bangladesh

Investigating determinants of HPV vaccine hesitancy, vaccine perceptions, and factors influencing acceptance.

Research Focus

HPV Vaccination Vaccine Hesitancy Adolescent Health Cervical Cancer Prevention

This study investigates the prevalence and determinants of HPV vaccine hesitancy among adolescent girls in Bangladesh. It explores awareness and perceptions of HPV infection and vaccination, individual and contextual factors influencing vaccine acceptance, parental and social influences, and concerns specific to vaccination.

Study Design

A mixed-methods design combining quantitative survey data with qualitative insights to provide both measurable evidence and deeper understanding of the experiences and perceptions shaping vaccine hesitancy.

Current Status

Manuscript in preparation, with ongoing work focused on analysis, interpretation, scientific writing, and preparation for peer-reviewed journal submission.

Expected Contribution

The findings are intended to inform more effective HPV vaccination communication strategies, strengthen school-based vaccination programs, enhance community engagement, and support policy development for cervical cancer prevention in Bangladesh.

07 — Future Research Direction

Preparing for Doctoral Research

As I move toward doctoral training, I aim to expand my research collaborations and contribute to the growing evidence base on HPV vaccination, health equity, maternal and child health, climate change and health, and humanitarian health systems.

I hope to build on my current research experience through advanced training in epidemiology, implementation science, behavioral science, health systems research, and climate and health.

My long-term goal is to develop an independent research career that connects rigorous scientific investigation with public health policy and implementation. I aim to contribute evidence that is relevant to communities, useful to policymakers, and capable of informing interventions that improve health outcomes and equity.

My research portfolio is still developing, but each project represents an important step toward becoming an independent public health researcher. I am committed to building a research career grounded in rigorous methods, ethical practice, collaboration, and meaningful contribution to public health.

Ultimately, I hope my research will move beyond publication to contribute to better decisions, stronger health systems, and measurable improvements in the health and well-being of vulnerable populations.
