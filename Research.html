Research

Research & Areas of Interest

My research focuses on improving population health through interdisciplinary, evidence-based approaches that address some of the most pressing public health challenges in low- and middle-income countries.

I am particularly interested in understanding how behavioral, social, environmental, and health system factors influence health outcomes and in translating research findings into effective policies and interventions.

My work integrates epidemiology, implementation science, behavioral science, and health systems research to improve vaccination programs, strengthen maternal and child health services, and enhance the resilience of healthcare systems in the face of emerging global challenges.

Research Theme 01

HPV Vaccination, Vaccine Confidence & Cervical Cancer Prevention

Research Overview

My primary research focuses on HPV vaccination and cervical cancer prevention, with particular emphasis on vaccine confidence, health behavior, and implementation strategies in resource-limited settings. I am interested in understanding the individual, social, cultural, and health system determinants that influence HPV vaccine acceptance among adolescents and their families.

My goal is to generate evidence that supports equitable vaccination policies, strengthens public trust in immunization programs, and contributes to the global elimination of cervical cancer.

Current Research

Current Research Project
HPV Vaccine Hesitancy among Adolescent Girls in Bangladesh

My current research project investigates the prevalence and determinants of vaccine hesitancy among secondary school students. The study explores knowledge of HPV infection and cervical cancer, perceptions of vaccine safety and effectiveness, parental influence, social norms, and contextual barriers affecting vaccine uptake.

The findings are intended to inform school-based vaccination strategies, health education initiatives, and policy recommendations that improve HPV vaccination coverage among adolescent girls.

Research Objectives

  • 01

    Assess knowledge and awareness of HPV infection, cervical cancer, and HPV vaccination.

  • 02

    Identify behavioral, social, and contextual determinants of HPV vaccine hesitancy.

  • 03

    Examine the influence of parents, teachers, healthcare providers, and social media on vaccination decisions.

  • 04

    Develop evidence-based recommendations to strengthen school-based vaccination programs.

  • 05

    Support national efforts to improve cervical cancer prevention through increased vaccine confidence and equitable access.

Research Theme 02

Climate Change & Health

Research Overview

Climate change is one of the greatest public health challenges of the twenty-first century, disproportionately affecting vulnerable populations in low-resource settings. My research interest lies in understanding how climate-related hazards—including rising temperatures, floods, cyclones, air pollution, and changing patterns of infectious diseases—affect maternal, reproductive, and child health.

I seek to generate evidence that supports climate-resilient health systems and informs policies that protect populations most at risk.

Research Interests

Climate change and maternal health
Pregnancy outcomes associated with extreme heat
Air pollution and reproductive health
Nutrition and food security under changing climate conditions
Floods and climate-related displacement
Climate-sensitive infectious diseases
Mental health consequences of climate-related disasters
One Health and Planetary Health approaches
Health system resilience and climate adaptation

Future Research Agenda

  • Climate-resilient maternal and reproductive healthcare services.
  • Climate impacts on pregnancy, newborn, and child health.
  • Early warning systems for climate-sensitive diseases.
  • Vector-borne disease surveillance and prevention.
  • Climate adaptation policies for vulnerable communities.
  • Environmental epidemiology and health equity.
  • Sustainable healthcare systems and low-carbon public health interventions.

Through interdisciplinary collaboration, I aim to contribute to global efforts addressing the health impacts of climate change while promoting equitable and sustainable development.

Research Theme 03

Maternal & Child Health

Research Overview

Maternal and child health remains a cornerstone of my research and professional practice. I am committed to improving the health and well-being of women, newborns, children, and adolescents through research that strengthens healthcare delivery, promotes preventive services, and reduces health inequities.

My interests span the continuum of care—from adolescence and preconception to pregnancy, childbirth, the postnatal period, and early childhood—with a particular focus on underserved and humanitarian settings.

Future Research Vision

By combining epidemiological research, implementation science, and policy evaluation, I hope to contribute to more equitable, resilient, and sustainable healthcare systems.

Research Philosophy

Evidence That Leads to Impact

I believe that impactful public health research extends beyond generating scientific evidence—it should improve lives, inform policy, and strengthen health systems.

My research philosophy is grounded in scientific rigor, ethical practice, interdisciplinary collaboration, and meaningful engagement with communities. I am committed to conducting research that addresses real-world health challenges, advances health equity, and translates knowledge into practical solutions.

Through collaboration with clinicians, researchers, policymakers, and international partners, I aim to contribute to evidence-informed decision-making that improves the health of women, children, adolescents, and vulnerable populations worldwide.
